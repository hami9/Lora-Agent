# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers share responsibility for respectful project spaces. Discuss the behavior of a design, test or patch. Give people room to clarify mistakes. Harassment, threats, targeted insults, discrimination and publishing private information violate this policy in issues, discussions, reviews and other project spaces.

A participant can ask a maintainer for a private contact route without posting identifying details in a public issue. Send the relevant URLs, dates, impact and requested privacy level privately. If the complaint concerns the receiving maintainer, request another maintainer where one is available. Keep details limited to the people handling the report.

Maintainers may hide abusive content, ask for a correction, restrict participation or remove a participant according to severity and repeated behavior. Explain a moderation decision privately where practical and hear material corrections before closing the report.
